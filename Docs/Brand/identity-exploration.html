The brief, distilled

Cirrus clouds are thin, high and fast. So is a well-compressed file.

Cirrus forms at 6–12 km, made of ice crystals rather than droplets, stretched into fine streaks by high-altitude wind. Those are the useful ideas: lightness (almost no mass), height (premium, above the noise), and streaks that converge toward the horizon. The trap is the puffy cumulus cloud, which every cloud-storage product already owns. All four directions avoid it.

Six cirrus streaks sweep across a high-altitude dawn sky and converge on one bright point: a lot of material made into something small and luminous. The gradient runs from stratospheric blue down to a warm horizon, which gives the icon its own colour in the Dock without leaning on a symbol.

Recommendation

Convergence, executed with Vapour-frame restraint

Direction A is the only one that carries the whole idea: cirrus streaks (identity), convergence (compression), a single bright point (the picture you keep). It also gives the app a motion language rather than just a logo. From B, it borrows discipline: white line work, no extra symbols, a dedicated small-size icon. The rest of this page shows A as a complete system.

Why this split

Inside the app, SF Pro is the right choice. It's what makes a Mac app feel native, and Apple's own apps never bundle a custom UI face. The brand typeface, Hanken Grotesk Light (open-source, OFL licence), only appears in the wordmark, the About window and marketing. Its wide, light letterforms echo thin cloud. In the app, the wordmark ships as a vector image, so no font is installed. Numbers use tabular figures everywhere so sizes and percentages line up.

System · motion

Streaks that move with the work

Waiting for a drop

Streaks drift slowly behind the drop zone. On hover with a file, they brighten and lean toward the cursor.

Compressing

Progress is drawn as the streaks extending toward the point. Each stage (analysing, searching, encoding, verifying) is a visible segment, with ETA alongside.

Done

The streaks settle into a single line and the point warms to Dawn, next to the size saved. One quiet moment, then it's still. Everything respects Reduce Motion.
